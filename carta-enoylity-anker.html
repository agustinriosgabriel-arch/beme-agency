Subject
Final Extension Notice — Anker SOLIX Storm Season Marketing Campaign

To the Enoylity Media Creations team,

We are writing with an update on the Anker SOLIX Storm Season Marketing Campaign and the path forward.

On May 12, 2026, we met with the Anker brand team to discuss this campaign. Based on the current state of delivery — the missed posting window of May 2–3, the gap between our feedback and your revised cut on May 12, and the fact that the submitted version still does not meet the approved brief — the brand's initial position was full cancellation.

We have managed to push back on that decision and secure one final extension. The terms below are firm and non-negotiable.

1 New Delivery Deadline

A new draft must be delivered by end of day, May 14, 2026 (8:00 PM EST). This is the only extension being granted.

2 What the New Draft Must Include

The revised cut must respect every requirement previously communicated, including the original brief (re-attached for reference) and in particular:

  • Usage demonstration scenes: the Anker SOLIX F3800 / F3800 Plus must be clearly shown on camera powering multiple essential appliances at the same time — e.g., refrigerator, washing machine, hair dryer, kettle, computer, lighting, etc. Voice-over claims that the unit "is connected" or "is working" while only cables are visible will not be accepted. The product itself, in operative use, must be on screen.
  • All must-do shots specified in the brief must be included.
  • All Mandatory Mentions set out in Section 5 of the Agreement must be present in both the integration and the description box:
    "Anker SOLIX is the only backup power you need."
    "Search Anker SOLIX on Google or Amazon or check out the links below for more info."
    "100 Preppers."
  • Content Direction and Big Idea — "Backup Early. Storm Ready." / "See how I prepare for storm season with Anker SOLIX" — must drive the creative.
  • Tracking link and hashtags provided by the brand must be included in the description box.

3 Consequences of Failing to Deliver

If the draft is not delivered by end of day on May 14, 2026, or if it is delivered but fails to address the points above, the following will apply automatically and without further notice:

  • Termination of the Agreement for Default under Clause 11.
  • Return of the US$3,000 advance payment within three (3) business days, pursuant to Clause 9 of the Agreement.
  • Return of the Anker SOLIX F3800 / F3800 Plus product within three (3) business days, pursuant to Clause 14 of the Agreement. Beme Agency will cover return shipping.
  • Removal of Enoylity Technology (@enoylitytechnology) from future collaboration consideration with Anker and all brands within the same group, including but not limited to Anker SOLIX, Anker Charging, eufy, and soundcore.

4 Next Steps

Please confirm receipt of this notice and your commitment to the May 14 deadline by reply today, May 12, during labour hours. The re-attached brief contains everything needed to produce a compliant cut.

Path Forward

We would prefer to see this campaign close successfully. The path to that outcome is narrow, but we are keeping it open until end of day May 14, 2026, 8:00 PM EST.
